OpenBattle design · Level 1 · Decision

ADR-007: Measuring is not an action

Status
Proposed
Context
Measurements could be shared and recorded, or private and instant.
Decision
A measurement is a private look. It is not recorded and the other player does not see it.
Because
Players measure constantly; recording every look would flood the log. At a real table, a quick check isn't announced either.
Rejected
Recording measurements: noisy log. Showing every live measurement to the other player: distracting and chatty on the network. A "share this measurement" button can come later if wanted.
