ADR-027: Stats are counters and marks on things, and items give them structure

Status
Accepted Porter chose "add item" on 2026-10-17 after asking whether counters need structure. Accepted by Porter on 2026-10-17.
Context
A rules package needs each unit's stats. Some fit a counter or a mark on the unit (Quality 4, Defense 3, Tough 6, Stealth). Weapons don't: each has several values (range, attacks, AP, rules such as Blast(3)), and models in one unit carry different ones.
Decision
A new concept, item: a named thing a model carries, holding counters and marks, and able to hold other items. All rules data is counters and marks on things: army, unit, model and item. Counters stay single named numbers. Players can give units, models and items their counters, marks and items when bringing an army.
Because
One small idea, counters and marks on things, covers every stat; the screen and the log only ever show and change named numbers and on-off labels. It matches how army books list gear per model, and nesting covers games with deeper gear.
Rejected
Counters that hold records and lists: the screen and the log would have to understand any shape, the road the first attempt's package runtime took. Flattening weapons into unit counters like "Pulse Rifle attacks": breaks when models in a unit carry different gear. A rules profile kept only by the package: the game wouldn't replay or read without the package.
Affects
What a player can do, Item, Counter, Mark, Model, Record, Screen
