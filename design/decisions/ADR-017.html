OpenBattle design · Level 3 · Decision

ADR-017: Controls follow Tabletop Simulator

Status
Accepted Accepted by Porter on 2026-10-15.
Context
Porter's players know Tabletop Simulator, and its feel is the target (ADR-002).
Decision
Where Tabletop Simulator has a control for something, the screen uses the same mouse button or key. Where it has none (the ruler, carrying a whole unit), the screen picks one and shows it.
Because
Nothing to relearn for the people this is for.
Rejected
Our own control scheme: no gain worth the relearning.
Affects
Screen surface
