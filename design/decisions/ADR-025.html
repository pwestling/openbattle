ADR-025: Neutral markers, marks on terrain, and reserves

Status
Proposed Porter chose "add all three" on 2026-10-17, after reviewing the Grimdark Future game flow. The wording awaits his review.
Context
Walking a full Grimdark Future game through the design showed three things a hand-played game needs that the engine can't hold. Objective markers and deployment zones belong to no player, but every model is in a unit in an army. Cover, difficult and dangerous are per terrain piece, but marks only go on models, units, groups and players. Scout and Ambush units wait off the table and units ride in transports, but a model is only on the table or removed as a casualty.
Decision
A new concept, marker: a flat disc or rectangle owned by nobody. Marks and counters may go on terrain pieces and markers. A model may be set aside in reserve, kept apart from removed models, and placed on the table later. Measuring reaches a marker's edge and shows the distance across the table as well as the height. None of these carry rules meaning in the first version.
Because
Each is something players track by hand in every Grimdark Future game, so they help the first version reach its yardstick, and each later gives a rules package something to read. Melee reach is "2 inches across and 4 up", so the distance across the table is needed beside the height.
Rejected
Objectives as models in a pretend "neutral" army: it breaks the meaning of army and clutters the army panel. Separate concepts for an objective and a zone: both are flat shapes owned by nobody. A "carried by" link from a unit to its transport: a reserve plus a mark covers it until a package needs it. Line of sight: needed too, but it has its own design thread.
Affects
What a player can do, Marker, Mark, Counter, Model, Terrain, Measurement, Record, Measure, Screen
