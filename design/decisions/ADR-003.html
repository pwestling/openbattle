OpenBattle design · Level 1 · Decision

ADR-003: The log is the game; the table is worked out from it

Status
Accepted
Context
Every browser must hold the same game, survive reloads, and support undo and looking back.
Decision
A game is an ordered log of actions. The table at any point is the result of applying the log from the start up to that point.
Because
One ordered log gives undo, looking back, resuming and catching up without extra machinery, and makes disagreement easy to detect. This part of the first attempt worked.
Rejected
Sharing the table itself and merging changes: harder to keep identical, and no history.
Affects
log, action, table, Record
