OpenBattle design · Level 0 · Decision

ADR-002: The table is drawn flat, from above

Status
Proposed
Context
The table could be shown in 3D, as the first attempt did, or top-down.
Decision
Draw the table top-down. Models are base outlines with a label; terrain is a footprint with its height written on it.
Because
Measuring and dragging are easier and more exact flat. 3D brings camera controls, models to draw, performance work, and the expectation of line-of-sight checks. Heights are still recorded, so a 3D view can come later.
Rejected
A 3D table: more work in every direction, and not needed to finish a game.
