OpenBattle design · Level 0 · Decision

ADR-002: The table is 3D and should feel like a real one

Status
Accepted Replaces the rejected top-down proposal, per Porter's review on 2026-10-14. Accepted by Porter on 2026-10-14.
Context
Porter: recreating the feel of playing in real life, as Tabletop Simulator does, is a core design element.
Decision
A 3D table with a free camera. Models are picked up, carried and set down; dice are thrown and seen to land. On top of that feel, the help Tabletop Simulator lacks: exact rulers, unit moves, the log and undo. There is no general physics simulation: positions are exact and recorded, and dice animate to the result already decided.
Because
The feel is the point of the product. Physics results differ between browsers, so they can't be the shared truth; they can only be decoration.
Rejected
A flat top-down table (the first proposal): loses the feel. Full physics as in Tabletop Simulator: models topple and drift, and two browsers would disagree.
Affects
Feels like the real table, table, model, terrain, Screen
