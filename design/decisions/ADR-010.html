OpenBattle design · Level 2 · Decision

ADR-010: Measuring is its own module

Status
Accepted
Context
Distance geometry could live in the record, next to the positions.
Decision
A separate Measure module owns all geometry. The record stores positions and shapes but never computes distances.
Because
The record stays small and only concerned with history. Geometry is the most numerically fiddly code and is easier to test on its own.
Rejected
Geometry inside the record: the record grows a second job.
Affects
Measure, Record
