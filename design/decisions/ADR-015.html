OpenBattle design · Level 3 · Decision

ADR-015: NineteenTwenty kinds of action, each a physical or shared-rules change

Status
Accepted Accepted by Porter on 2026-10-15, with "bring army" changed to "add to army". A twentieth kind, lock terrain, added at Porter's request on 2026-10-16 (ADR-024).
Context
The record needs a fixed list of actions. The first attempt's list grew to over thirty kinds, plus scripts that produced more, and its format changed nine times in days.
Decision
NineteenTwenty kinds (fifteen, plus four for decks added by ADR-021 and lock terrain by ADR-024), listed on the Record surface. Adding to an army is one action, so a batch of units arrives whole or not at all, and it can happen at any point in the game (reinforcements, summoned units, models added to a unit). Models never move from one unit to another; attaching a leader is a group.
Because
Small enough to read in one table and to test completely. Each kind maps to something a player does with their hands at a real table, or to a phase, counter or mark.
Rejected
Separate actions per unit or model: the log fills with setup noise and a half-arrived batch is possible. Bringing the whole army once at the start: no way to add models mid-game. Changeable unit membership: breaks "every model is in exactly one unit" for no need OnePageRules has.
