OpenBattle design · Level 3 · Decision

ADR-015: Fifteen kinds of action, each a physical or shared-rules change

Status
Proposed
Context
The record needs a fixed list of actions. The first attempt's list grew to over thirty kinds, plus scripts that produced more, and its format changed nine times in days.
Decision
Fifteen kinds, listed on the Record surface. Bringing an army is one action, so a whole army arrives or none of it. Units don't change members after they arrive; attaching a leader is a group.
Because
Small enough to read in one table and to test completely. Each kind maps to something a player does with their hands at a real table, or to a phase, counter or mark.
Rejected
Separate actions per unit or model when bringing an army: the log fills with setup noise and a half-arrived army is possible. Changeable unit membership: breaks "every model is in exactly one unit" for no need OnePageRules has.
Affects
Record surface
