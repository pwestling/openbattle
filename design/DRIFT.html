Record surface

  • A shuffled draw picks cards by their place in the deck's current order, and that order isn't signed. A host that knows both values can first log a move card into the deck to choose which cards come up. Proposed fix, for Porter: the other player also signs a fingerprint of the deck's cards in their current order, and every browser checks it against the deck as it stood just before the draw. This needs Session to send the fingerprint with the seal, and the record to carry it in the draw.
