OpenBattle design · Level 1 · Concept

Log

Realizes: What a player can do Games survive One table
Purpose
The history of the game, which is also the game itself.
Meaning
Every action in a game, in order.
Invariants
  • The log only grows.
  • Undo is a new action that cancels the latest one; nothing is erased.
  • Applying the log from the start gives the table.
  • Anyone can look back at the table as it stood at any point.
Not
A chat.
