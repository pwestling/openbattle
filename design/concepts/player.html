Player

Realizes: What it is for One table
Purpose
Who is playing.
Meaning
A person in a game, with a name and a colour, who brings an army and takes actions.
Invariants
  • A player stays the same player after reloading or returning on the same browser.
  • Every action names the player who took it.
Not
A browser connection, a side or faction, or a spectator.
