OpenBattle design · Level 1 · Concept

Spectator

Realizes: What a player can do Games survive
Purpose
Let someone watch a game, and keep a copy of it.
Meaning
A person following a game who takes no actions.
Invariants
  • A spectator never takes actions.
  • A spectator sees the same table as the players.
  • A spectator can save the game to a file.
Not
A player, and never a host.
