Live view

Realizes: It feels like the real table
Status
Accepted Porter chose "Live view" on 2026-10-17, after noticing that what a player is doing was hidden from the opponent until it became an action. The pointer added at his request the same day. Accepted by Porter on 2026-10-17.
Purpose
Let the other players watch what someone is doing as they do it, as they would across a real table.
Meaning
What a player is in the middle of right now, before it becomes an action: where their pointer is on the table, as in Tabletop Simulator; models they are carrying and where, the ruler they are dragging, the sight lines they are checking, and the suggestion choices they have open, such as the targets they have picked.
Invariants
  • A live view never enters the log, is never undone and is not saved; a reload or a dropped connection simply ends it.
  • A live view changes nothing: only the action that follows counts, so nothing ever depends on one being honest.
  • Everyone in the game, spectators included, sees every player's live view; spectators have none of their own.
Not
An action. Not a measurement, which is the answer; a live view only shows someone measuring. Not the camera: where someone is looking stays their own.
