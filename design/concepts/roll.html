OpenBattle design · Level 1 · Concept

Roll

Realizes: What a player can do One table
Purpose
Dice both players trust equally.
Meaning
A number of dice of one size, thrown at once with an optional label, and the numbers that came up.
Invariants
  • A roll happens once, and its result never changes.
  • Both players see the same result.
  • Every roll is in the log.
Not
A rule such as "hits on 3+". The players read the dice.
