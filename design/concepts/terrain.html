OpenBattle design · Level 1 · Concept

Terrain

Realizes: What a player can do Feels like the real table
Purpose
The scenery that shapes the battle.
Meaning
A named piece of scenery on the table: either a simple solid (a box, a cylinder or a ramp) or a 3D mesh loaded from a file, sized in inches.
Invariants
  • Every piece has a shape, a size and a position.
  • A mesh piece looks the same in every browser: everyone has the same file, checked by its fingerprint.
  • In the first version terrain has no rules meaning, such as cover or impassable.
Not
A model.
