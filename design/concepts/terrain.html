OpenBattle design · Level 1 · Concept

Terrain

Realizes: What a player can do The players apply the rules
Purpose
The scenery that shapes the battle.
Meaning
A named rectangle or circle on the table, with a height in inches.
Invariants
  • Every piece has a shape, a size and a height.
  • The app gives terrain no rule meaning, such as cover or impassable.
Not
A model.
