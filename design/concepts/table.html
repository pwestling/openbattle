OpenBattle design · Level 1 · Concept

Table

Realizes: One table What a player can do
Purpose
The shared surface everything sits on.
Meaning
A rectangle measured in inches (60 × 44 unless the players choose otherwise) and everything on it at one moment: terrain, models and counters.
Invariants
  • The table at any moment is completely determined by the log up to that moment.
  • Both players see the same table at the same point in the log.
  • Positions and heights are in inches.
Not
The view on screen. Zoom, pan and selection belong to each player and are never shared.
Decisions: ADR-003
