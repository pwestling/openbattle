Marker

Realizes: What a player can do Players apply the rules
Status
Accepted Porter chose to add neutral markers on 2026-10-17, after the Grimdark Future game flow review. Accepted by Porter on 2026-10-17.
Purpose
Show the places a game cares about that belong to no player.
Meaning
A flat shape lying on the table, a disc or a rectangle sized in inches, with a name and a colour, such as an objective marker or a deployment zone.
Invariants
  • A marker belongs to no player and is in no army.
  • Placing, moving, resizing and removing a marker are actions, open to any player.
  • A marker is flat: models stand over it and it has no floors.
  • In the first version a marker has no rules meaning. Who holds an objective is shown with marks or counters on it.
Not
A model, which belongs to a unit in an army, or terrain, which is scenery with a shape and floors.
