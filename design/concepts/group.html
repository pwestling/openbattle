OpenBattle design · Level 1 · Concept

Group

Realizes: What a player can do Players apply the rules
Purpose
Units that are dealt with together, beyond a single unit.
Meaning
A named set of units or other groups, such as a unit with an attached leader, a squadron, or a whole army.
Invariants
  • A group belongs to one player.
  • A unit can be in several groups.
  • A group never contains itself, directly or through another group.
Not
A unit: a group holds units, a unit holds models.
