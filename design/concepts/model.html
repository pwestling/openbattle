OpenBattle design · Level 1 · Concept

Model

Realizes: What a player can do Feels like the real table
Purpose
One miniature on the table.
Meaning
A miniature on its base (round, oval or rectangular, sized in millimetres), at a position on the table in inches, facing a direction. Its body is a 3D mesh loaded from a file, or, without one, a simple stand-in of a given height.
Invariants
  • A model is either on the table, set aside in reserve (not yet on the table or off it for now, such as a unit waiting to arrive or riding in a transport), or set aside as removed; it is never deleted. A model in reserve can be placed on the table at any time (ADR-025).
  • A model's base size does not change during a game.
  • A model stands on the table or on a floor of a terrain piece, whichever the player chose. It never balances, tips or falls.
  • The body is the model's true shape, not decoration: its height, and later what it can see and be seen by, come from it.
  • A mesh body is the same in every browser: everyone has the same file, checked by its fingerprint.
Not
The rules meaning of its stats. A model carries items, and its stats are counters and marks on it, its unit and its items, with no built-in meaning.
