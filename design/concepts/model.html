OpenBattle design · Level 1 · Concept

Model

Realizes: What a player can do Feels like the real table
Purpose
One miniature on the table.
Meaning
A miniature represented by its base (round, oval or rectangular, sized in millimetres) and its height, at a position on the table in inches, facing a direction.
Invariants
  • A model is either on the table or set aside as removed; it is never deleted.
  • A model's base size does not change during a game.
Not
The miniature's sculpt or its rules profile.
