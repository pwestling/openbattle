OpenBattle design · Level 1 · Concept

Model

Realizes: What a player can do
Purpose
One miniature on the table.
Meaning
A base of a given size in millimetres (round, oval or rectangular), at a position on the table in inches, facing a direction.
Invariants
  • A model is either on the table or set aside as removed; it is never deleted.
  • A model's base size does not change during a game.
Not
The miniature's look or its rules profile.
