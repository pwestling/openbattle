OpenBattle design · Level 1 · Concept

Card

Realizes: What a player can do Dice and draws nobody can fix
Purpose
Game pieces that carry words or a picture, such as mission or objective cards.
Meaning
A named card with optional text and an optional picture. It is always in exactly one place: in a deck, in a player's hand, face up on the table, or in a discard pile.
Invariants
  • A card is in exactly one place at a time.
  • Moving a card is an action.
  • A card in someone's hand is shown face down to everyone else. This is hiding on screen only: the card is in every browser's copy of the log.
Not
A counter or a mark.
Decisions: ADR-008 ADR-021
