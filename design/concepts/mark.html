OpenBattle design · Level 1 · Concept

Mark

Realizes: What a player can do Players apply the rules
Purpose
Keep track of the yes-or-no states a game needs.
Meaning
A named label that is on or off for a model, unit, group, item, terrain piece, marker or player, such as "has moved", "pinned", or "cover" on a ruin, or "Rending" on a weapon.
Invariants
  • Changing a mark is an action.
  • The engine gives no mark a built-in meaning; a rules package may read one and suggest from it (ADR-029).
Not
A counter.
