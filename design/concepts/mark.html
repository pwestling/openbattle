OpenBattle design · Level 1 · Concept

Mark

Realizes: What a player can do Players apply the rules
Purpose
Keep track of the yes-or-no states a game needs.
Meaning
A named label that is on or off for a model, unit, group, item, terrain piece, marker or player, such as "has moved" or "pinned", "pinned", or "cover" on a ruin, or "Rending" on a weapon.
Invariants
  • Changing a mark is an action.
  • In the first version no mark has a built-in meaning.
Not
A counter.
