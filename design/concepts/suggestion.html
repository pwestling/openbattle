OpenBattle design · Level 1 · Concept

Suggestion

Realizes: Players apply the rules; a package helps Rules package
Status
Proposed Drafted on 2026-10-17 for the Grimdark Future package, after Porter asked to start one. Placed over its unit, opening into choices, with hints such as weapon range: Porter's flow of 2026-10-17.
Purpose
Let a rules package help without taking the game out of the players' hands.
Meaning
One next step a package offers one player: a sentence saying what and why, such as "Scouts shoot Orc Boyz: 10 attacks, hitting on 4+", and either the actions it would take or the roll it would make. A suggestion sits where it applies, such as over the unit it is about, and may open into the next choices, one at a time: Activate opens into Hold, Advance, Rush and Charge, and Shoot asks for a target for each weapon type. It may come with hints drawn lightly on the table for that player, such as how far the unit's weapons reach. Choosing along the way changes nothing; the other players watch it as part of the chooser's live view, and only the step that takes actions or rolls counts.
Invariants
  • Showing a suggestion changes nothing; only taking it does.
  • Taking a suggestion is exactly the same as taking its actions by hand, each carrying the suggestion's sentence as its note.
  • A suggestion is for the player whose step it is, which is usually whoever has priority. Others see it too, so everyone knows what the game is waiting on.
  • The same log, and the same choices picked so far, give the same suggestions in every browser.
  • A suggestion only offers choices the rules allow, such as at most two targets when shooting; anything else is still possible by hand.
Not
A rules check: a suggestion never refuses a move, and ignoring it is always allowed. Not priority, which says who the game waits on; a suggestion says what they might do.
