OpenBattle design · Level 1 · Concept

Priority

Realizes: What a player can do Players apply the rules
Status
Proposed Asked for by Porter on 2026-10-17: "we also need a concept of Priority, i.e. who is expected to act next". This wording awaits his review.
Purpose
Everyone knows who the game is waiting on.
Meaning
The player or players expected to act next, with an optional short note of what for, such as "roll blocks for 4 hits". It moves back and forth inside one turn: the active player has it, the opponent gets it to block or strike back, and it comes back.
Invariants
  • Changing priority is an action.
  • Priority never stops anyone acting: the app still refuses no move, and a player without priority can act.
  • Everyone sees who has priority, and the players who have it are told it is their move.
  • In the first version players hand priority over by hand; a rules package may later hand it over for them.
Not
Whose turn it is, which is part of the phase and changes only between turns. Not a lock on the table.
