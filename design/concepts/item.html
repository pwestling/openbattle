OpenBattle design · Level 1 · Concept

Item

Realizes: What a player can do Players apply the rules
Status
Accepted Porter chose "add item" on 2026-10-17, with items able to hold items. Accepted by Porter on 2026-10-17.
Purpose
Give a model's gear its own numbers and states, so a game's stats have the shape its army books give them.
Meaning
A named thing a model carries, such as a weapon or wargear, holding counters and marks: a Pulse Rifle with Range 30, Attacks 1 and AP 1, and the mark "Rending". An item may hold other items, such as a vehicle's hardpoint holding a weapon.
Invariants
  • Every item is carried by exactly one model or held by exactly one other item, and no item holds itself, directly or through another item.
  • Adding, removing, moving or renaming an item is an action.
  • An item is not drawn on the table and has no position; it goes where its model goes.
  • The engine gives no item a built-in meaning; a rules package may read one and suggest from it (ADR-029).
Not
A model or a card. Not a structured value inside a counter: counters stay single named numbers, and structure comes from what carries them.
