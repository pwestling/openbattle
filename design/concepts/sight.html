OpenBattle design · Level 1 · Concept

Line of sight

Realizes: What a player can do Feels like the real table Players apply the rules; packages can help later
Status
Proposed Drafted 2026-10-17 from Porter's ask for line-of-sight handling, so that rules packages (OnePageRules first) can use it (ADR-026).
Purpose
Answer "can this model see that one?" the way a player would by crouching down to the table, but exactly.
Meaning
Whether a straight line can be drawn from some part of one model's body to some part of another model's body, or to a point, without passing through anything in the way; how much of the target can be seen; and which pieces are in the way of the rest. What can be in the way is the body of any terrain piece or other model, at its true shape: a mesh, a simple solid, or a stand-in body. Bases are not part of it: they neither see, nor are seen, nor block.
Invariants
  • The same question gets the same answer in every browser.
  • If one model sees another, the other sees it, when the same pieces count as in the way.
  • Looking changes nothing and is not an action.
  • Whoever asks may say which pieces don't count as in the way, such as a wood that is cover but not a sight blocker, or the looker's own unit.
Not
A rules judgement. It says what can be seen and what is in the way; whether that means a shot is allowed or a unit is in cover is for the players, or a rules package. Not a measurement: it says nothing about range.
