OpenBattle design · Level 1 · Concept

Counter

Realizes: What a player can do
Purpose
Keep track of the numbers a game needs.
Meaning
A named number on a unit, a model, a player or the game: wounds left, victory points, the round.
Invariants
  • Changing a counter is an action.
  • The app gives no counter a meaning.
Not
A rules profile or stat line.
