OpenBattle design · Level 1 · Concept

Deck

Realizes: What a player can do Dice and draws nobody can fix
Purpose
Draws nobody can rig.
Meaning
A named face-down pile of cards, belonging to a player or to the game, that cards are drawn from at random.
Invariants
  • A deck has no order decided in advance: each draw picks at random from the cards left.
  • Nobody, the host included, can choose which card is drawn without the others being able to tell, the same way as a roll.
  • Every draw is in the log.
  • Shuffling cards back in just returns them to the pile.
Not
A hand, and not an ordered stack you can look through.
Decisions: ADR-008 ADR-021
