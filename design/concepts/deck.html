OpenBattle design · Level 1 · Concept

Deck

Realizes: What a player can do Dice and draws nobody can fix
Purpose
A pile of cards to draw from, either in a set order or shuffled so nobody can rig the draw.
Meaning
A named face-down pile of cards, belonging to a player or to the game. It is either ordered, a stack drawn from the top with cards put back on the top, the bottom or a given position, or shuffled, a pile drawn from at random.
Invariants
  • An ordered deck's order is known and in the log; drawing takes the top card.
  • A shuffled deck has no order decided in advance: each draw picks at random from the cards left, and nobody, the host included, can choose which card is drawn without the others being able to tell, the same way as a roll. As with a roll, the host may draw alone when no other player is connected, and the draw is marked as drawn alone (ADR-023).
  • Shuffling turns an ordered deck into a shuffled one. A shuffled deck never becomes ordered again, since that would mean deciding its order.
  • Every draw is in the log.
  • A card put back into a shuffled deck just returns to the pile.
Not
A hand, and not an ordered stack you can look through.
Decisions: ADR-008 ADR-021
