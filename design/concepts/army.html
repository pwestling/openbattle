OpenBattle design · Level 1 · Concept

Army

Realizes: What a player can do Whose content we ship
Purpose
What a player brings to the game.
Meaning
A player's top-level group: every unit they brought, typed or pasted in, named however they like.
Invariants
  • A player has one army in a game.
  • Every unit is in exactly one army.
  • The app never checks an army against a game's rules.
  • The first version ships no army data; players type or paste their own. With a rules package on, a list in its game's form, such as an Army Forge export, brings its stats as counters, marks and items.
Not
A checked army list with points, and not a faction.
