OpenBattle design · Level 1 · Concept

Army

Realizes: What a player can do No publisher content The players apply the rules
Purpose
What a player brings to the game.
Meaning
A player's list of units, typed or pasted in, named however the player likes.
Invariants
  • An army belongs to one player.
  • The app never checks an army against any game's rules.
  • No army content ships with the app.
Not
A checked army list with points, and not a faction.
