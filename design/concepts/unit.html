OpenBattle design · Level 1 · Concept

Unit

Realizes: What a player can do Players apply the rules
Purpose
Models that act together.
Meaning
A named group of models, moved and tracked together.
Invariants
  • Every model belongs to exactly one unit; a lone model is a unit of one.
  • Every unit is in exactly one army.
Not
A single model.
