Screen surface

  • Keys picked where Tabletop Simulator has none, as ADR-017 allows, and listed on screen under Controls: hold Tab for the ruler; Space or right-click pins a waypoint; T looks straight down and H goes back to your edge; Escape puts back what you carry (src/screen/table/gestures.ts).
  • Decks and hands: F flips the card under the pointer between face up and face down, as in Tabletop Simulator. The surface doesn't list flipping (src/screen/table/gestures.ts, flip).
  • Decks and hands: putting a card on the bottom of a deck uses Shift as the modifier key.
  • Picking up: Shift-click adds a model to the selection or takes it out.
  • Panels: new decks are made in a Decks panel beside Army and Terrain. It also has Draw, Draw face up and Shuffle buttons for each deck. The surface's list of panels doesn't name it (src/screen/panels/decks.tsx).
  • Army: the list format is one unit per line, such as 5x Scouts 25mm, Captain 40mm 1.5" or Tank 100x60mm rect 2.5". The count defaults to 1, the base to 25 mm round, and the stand-in height to 1.2″ (src/screen/army-list.ts).
  • Where things go: new models stand in rows along your table edge, new terrain in a row across the middle of the table, and new decks just off your edge with their discard pile beside them.
  • Connection banner: it also says when this browser won't keep the game, from the snapshot's kept (src/screen/game.tsx, Banner).
  • Log: Undo shows beside your action only when it is the latest uncancelled action in the game, because the record's undo cancels the latest uncancelled action.
  • Start page: a saved file opens through Archive's save and then Session's enter, as the game's first player. No new operation is needed.
